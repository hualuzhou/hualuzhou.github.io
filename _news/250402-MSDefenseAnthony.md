I’m proud to share that my first master’s student at the University of Georgia Griffin Campus UGA Food Science and Technology, Anthony Suryamiharja, successfully defended his final thesis today!

Anthony is a smart, hardworking, and multi-tasking student. He completed multiple research projects on the "Post pH-Driven Method (PPD)" and also actively competed in 3MT, College Bowl, and product development competitions, earning impressive achievements.

I’m so proud of everything Anthony has accomplished, and wish him all the best for a bright and exciting future! 🌟

Huge thanks to the committee members, Prof. Casimir Akoh and Prof. Koushik Adhikari, for their support and guidance throughout Anthony’s journey.
