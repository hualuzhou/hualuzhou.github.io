Lu attended the AOCS Annual Meeting & Expo and chaired a session on Emulsions and Dispersed Systems for the EAT Division with Maria Lidia Herrera and Juan A. D. She also served as a judge for the student poster competition for the PCP Division, following an invitation from Nandika Bandara.

The meeting was also a special moment to celebrate Truong Giang Vu, now a Ph.D. student at OSU, who won both First Prize and the People's Choice Award in the EAT Division. Congratulations to Giang on this well-deserved recognition.

Lu shared that AOCS continues to be a wonderful opportunity to hear excellent presentations, reconnect with friends, meet new colleagues, and contribute to the broader community.
